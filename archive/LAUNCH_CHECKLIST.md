# Selah.fm — Curated Launch Plan

**Status:** ⬜ Not started  
**Priority:** 🔴 HIGHEST — single most important action for the platform  
**Effort:** ~20 hours manual outreach  
**Goal:** 5 artists + 20 creators → 5 live campaigns with real budgets → proof the marketplace works

---

## Phase 1: Find 5 Artists (10h)

### Criteria
- 1K-10K followers on TikTok or Instagram
- Active content creator (posts at least 1x/week)
- Has at least 1 song on Spotify/Apple Music
- Responsive to DMs or email
- Genre: anything except very niche (we need broad creator appeal)

### Outreach Script — Instagram DM

```
Hey [name]! Love your track [track name] — the [genre] vibe is fire 🔥

I'm running Selah.fm, a platform where creators make TikToks/Reels for your songs and you only pay for verified views. No upfront cost, you set the CPM rate.

Would you be down to be one of our first 5 featured artists? We'd set up a campaign with a $20-100 budget. Happy to explain more if interested!

— Robert-Jan
```

### Outreach Script — Email

```
Subject: Feature your music on Selah.fm (no upfront cost)

Hi [name],

I'm Robert-Jan, founder of Selah.fm — a marketplace where content creators make TikTok/Reels/Shorts for your music and you pay only for verified views.

We're launching with 5 featured artists and I'd love to include you. Here's how it works:

1. You upload your track and set a CPM rate ($5-30 per 1K views)
2. Creators submit videos featuring your song
3. You approve the ones you like
4. You pay only for verified views — no risk, no upfront cost

We'll seed the campaign with a $20-100 budget to attract creators. You keep 100% of the promotional value.

Interested? I can set everything up in under 10 minutes.

Best,
Robert-Jan
Founder, Selah.fm
https://selah.fm
```

## Phase 3: Launch Sprint (4h)

### Timeline
- **Day 1-3:** Find + onboard 5 artists (set up campaigns with $20-100 budgets)
- **Day 3-5:** Find + onboard 20 creators
- **Day 5-7:** Sprint week — creators make content, artists approve
- **Day 7-10:** Review results, document case studies, write blog posts
- **Day 10-14:** Measure, iterate, plan v2

### Setup Process (per artist)
1. Create artist account at selah.fm/onboarding?role=artist
2. Upload track (link from Spotify/Apple Music)
3. Set CPM rate ($10-50/1M views recommended)
4. Set budget ($20-100)
5. Write campaign brief (1-2 sentences about desired video style)
6. Share campaign link with creators

### Success Metrics
| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Submissions per campaign | 5+ |
| Total views across all campaigns | 50,000+ |
| Creators who earn at least $1 | 10+ |
| Case studies produced | 2+ |
| New user signups from campaign | 20+ |
